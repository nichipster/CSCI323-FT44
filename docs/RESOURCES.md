# Project Resources & Links

## Papers & Theory

### Primary Paper
- **AlphaGo Nature Paper:** Silver et al. (2016) - Located in `/mnt/project/nature16961.pdf`

### Supplementary Papers
1. **MCTS Survey:** Browne et al. (2012) "A Survey of Monte Carlo Tree Search Methods"
   - https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6145622

2. **CNN for Go:** Clark & Storkey (2015) "Training Deep CNNs to Play Go"
   - http://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/clark15.pdf

3. **Deep Learning & Go Book:** Pumperla & Ferguson (2019)
   - https://github.com/maxpumperla/deep_learning_and_the_game_of_go
